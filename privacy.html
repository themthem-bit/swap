Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

About this policy

This page explains what Swap (“the bot”) stores about people in the Discord server it is set up for (“the Server”), why it stores it, and for how long. I run the bot and decide what it stores. The Terms of Service apply as well.

The bot only sees what Discord sends it about the Server, plus whatever you type into its forms and ticket channels. It never asks for passwords or payment details. It ignores every other server it gets added to and stores nothing from them.

What is stored

  • Account details. Your Discord user ID, username or display name, and avatar link. These sit next to every record below so staff can tell who it concerns.
  • Staff applications. Your ID and username, your answers (these can include your age, country, time zone, languages and anything else you choose to write), when you started and submitted, and the decision along with who made it. Kept until staff delete the application.
  • Tickets. For application and support tickets: the type, status and times, plus a text transcript of the ticket channel that is posted to a private staff channel when the ticket closes. Kept until staff delete them.
  • Moderation records. Warnings, timeouts, kicks and bans, each with your ID, the moderator’s ID, the reason, the length, the date and any notes. Temporary bans and the list of people blocked from applying are kept too. They are used to apply the rules consistently and to look back at old cases, and they stay until staff remove them.
  • Message copies. So that deleted and edited messages can be logged, the bot keeps a copy of each message sent in the Server: its ID, channel, author, text, attachment links, stickers and mentions. Copies are deleted automatically after 7 days by default. Staff can set this anywhere from 1 to 90 days.
  • Attachment copies. Files posted in the Server are saved briefly so staff can see what was deleted. The default is 24 hours, staff can raise it to as much as 30 days, and the total size is capped. Older copies are deleted automatically.
  • Activity logs. The bot posts to private staff channels when members join or leave, change their nickname, roles, username or avatar, are banned or unbanned, create invites or move between voice channels, and when channels, roles or server settings change. These posts stay in Discord until staff delete them.
  • Levelling. Your ID, name, XP, message count and time spent in voice channels. Kept until staff reset or remove it.
  • Honeypots and anti-nuke. When someone posts in a honeypot channel, the bot records their ID, username, the message and the action it took. The anti-nuke system records incidents, and the roles it temporarily removes from someone so they can be restored. Kept until staff remove them.
  • Settings. Role, channel and member IDs used in the rules for who can use which command.
  • Direct messages. The bot may send you a DM, for example about a warning, kick, ban, application update or being blocked from applying.

Who can see it

Only me and the members of the Server’s staff who have the right permissions. Application answers, moderation records and logs are not shown to other members.

Your data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or profiling. It is shared with nobody except Discord, the hosting provider that runs the bot, and the authorities when the law requires it.

Storage and security

Data is held in database files on the server or hosting service where the bot runs, and in the private Discord channels used for logs and transcripts. The bot’s login token is kept private. I can make a backup copy of the database, and I keep any backup private.

The host may be in a different country from you, so your data may be processed there. No system is completely secure, so I can’t guarantee that data will never be exposed.

Your rights

You can ask what the bot holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Use the contact details below.

Moderation records may be kept even after a request when they are needed to protect the Server from abuse or to settle a dispute. Leaving the Server doesn’t delete your records automatically, so ask if you want them removed.

Anything that lives on Discord itself is handled by Discord under its Privacy Policy.

Children

The bot isn’t meant for anyone under the minimum age Discord requires, which is 13 in most countries. If I learn that it holds data from someone under that age, I will delete it.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when I do, and I’ll announce any significant change in the Server.

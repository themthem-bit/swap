Staff applications

Applying is voluntary and doesn’t give you a right to a position. The Server’s staff decide on applications. They can accept, decline or block anyone from applying, and they don’t have to give a reason.

Swap only records your answers so staff can read them. It doesn’t decide anything itself. The Privacy Policy explains how your answers are handled.

Moderation and automatic actions

Authorised staff use Swap to warn, time out, kick and ban members and to keep a record of what was done.

Some actions happen without anyone pressing a button: the word and invite filters, honeypot channels (channels where posting triggers an automatic action), and the anti-nuke system, which can take permissions away from someone who mass-deletes channels or mass-bans members.

Automatic actions are sometimes wrong. If you think one was, contact the Server’s staff to appeal. Staff make the final decision. I don’t review individual moderation decisions.
